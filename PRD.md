# Product Requirements Document (PRD)
## EM's Burgers — Website Mockup

**Prepared by:** saneeedotin
**Client:** EM's Burgers, Chembur, Mumbai
**Document type:** Mockup / prototype (not a production build — no backend, no real payments)

---

## 1. Background

EM's Burgers is a newly opened burger cafe in Chembur Camp, Mumbai (20, Acharaya Udyog Complex, Koliwada, Borla Road, Chembur). It currently has no website — its only online presence is Instagram and food-delivery listings (Zomato/Swiggy/District). The owner isn't ready to commission a full website yet, so this project delivers a clickable, visually finished **mockup** to show what the site could look like, which will later be handed to a coding agent to turn into a production build.

## 2. Goals

1. Give the client a concrete, on-brand visual of their future website to help them commit to going live.
2. Establish an online presence / digital menu showcase.
3. Drive delivery orders by linking out to Zomato/Swiggy.
4. Introduce a **QR-code-based loyalty program**: buy 9 burgers, get the 10th free.

## 3. Target audience

- Local Chembur residents and office-goers browsing for a place to eat or order from.
- Existing customers scanning a table/counter QR code to check their loyalty points.
- People discovering the cafe via Instagram/Google and wanting menu + location before visiting.

## 4. Scope — Pages

| Page | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Hero intro, brand story hook, highlight dishes, loyalty program teaser, CTA to order/visit |
| **Menu** | Full menu by category with images, names, short descriptions, prices |
| **About** | Cafe story, ambience/vibe, what makes EM's different |
| **Contact** | Address, map embed, phone, hours, Instagram link, delivery links |

## 5. Feature requirements

### 5.1 Home page
- Hero section with logo, tagline, and a hero food shot
- "Signature picks" strip (e.g. UFO Burger, Pull Me Up Burger, Signature Burger, Destroyed Fries)
- Loyalty program banner ("Buy 9, get the 10th free") linking to the loyalty page/section
- Order-now buttons linking to Zomato/Swiggy
- Footer with address, hours, social links

### 5.2 Menu page
- Categorized menu: Burgers (veg/non-veg), Sides (fries, mac & cheese bites, chicken tenders), Drinks/Mocktails
- Each item: name, short description, price (placeholder until client provides final pricing/photos)
- Veg/non-veg indicator (standard Indian green/red dot)
- Visual placeholders clearly marked as "sample image" until real photography is supplied

### 5.3 About page
- Brand story block (placeholder copy — client to confirm real story)
- Ambience gallery (placeholder images matching the cafe's vibe: casual, vibrant, hangout-friendly)
- Highlights: fresh in-house buns, veg & non-veg options, dine-in + takeaway + delivery

### 5.4 Contact page
- Address: 20, Acharaya Udyog Complex, Koliwada, Borla Road, Chembur, Mumbai
- Hours: 12:00 PM – 11:00 PM daily (placeholder — confirm with client)
- Phone/WhatsApp click-to-call
- Embedded Google Map
- Links: Instagram (@emschembur), Zomato, Swiggy

### 5.5 Loyalty program (QR-based)
- A dedicated loyalty section/page reachable via a QR code (printed at the counter/on receipts)
- Mockup shows a simple punch-card UI: 9 filled slots + 1 "free" slot
- For this mockup phase: **static/demo UI only** — no real scanning, auth, or points backend (flagged as a future build item in the TRD)

## 6. Out of scope (for this mockup phase)
- Real online ordering / payment integration
- Backend, database, user accounts
- Real loyalty point tracking (QR scan → points logic)
- CMS for the client to edit menu/prices themselves
- Final photography and copywriting (placeholders used, clearly marked)

## 7. Assumptions & open items
- Exact menu prices are not yet confirmed — placeholder prices will be used and marked as such.
- Real dish/ambience photography has not been supplied yet — stock/placeholder imagery will be used, styled to match the described vibe ("classy and welcoming," "cool, vibrant vibes," "cute aesthetic").
- Operating hours (12 PM–11 PM) pulled from third-party listings — needs client confirmation.
- Loyalty program mechanics (how a scan is verified, staff-side vs customer-side) are undefined beyond "buy 9, get 10th free" — to be scoped later with the client.

## 8. Success criteria for this deliverable
- Client can click through Home → Menu → About → Contact and get a realistic feel for the finished site.
- Visual identity (colors, tone) is instantly recognizable as EM's Burgers.
- Loyalty concept is understandable at a glance from the mockup alone.
